# Growth plan and weekly review

Source: research report "Explained in 60 growth plan" (Nov 2026). All targets are derived estimates; re-baseline at week 13 with the channel's own median.

## Key facts (reported)
- Full YPP for new applicants from Feb 1, 2027: 1,000 subs + 20M Shorts views in 90 days, or 8,000 long-form watch hours in 365 days. Shorts pool revenue only while holding 10M Shorts views per rolling 90 days.
- Lower tier (fan funding, no ad revenue): 500 subs + 3 uploads + 3M Shorts views in 90 days.
- Median US Shorts RPM about $0.33 per 1,000 views. Early money = sponsorships at about $10 to $25 per 1,000 views, plus affiliate.
- New-channel median: about 1,300 views per Short in 30 days; 20.9% of Shorts pass 10K; 48% of channels get a 10K Short within their first 10.
- Viewed vs swiped: under 60% performs poorly, 70 to 90% performs well.

## Cadence
- **Launch sprint, Nov 2 to 9, 2026 (owner decision):** 4 Shorts per day, an A/B test of the format. 2 per day two-host podcast (ElevenLabs), 2 per day single-host explainer (Kokoro, like 002). Slots 08:00, 12:00, 17:00, 20:00 ET, with formats swapped between even and odd days so time of day does not bias the result. The Nov 9 review compares the formats on views per Short, viewed vs swiped, average percentage viewed and subs per 1,000 views, then sets the cadence and format mix from Nov 10. The weekly target rows below are from the original 3 per week plan and get re-baselined on Nov 9.
- Weeks 1 to 4: 3 Shorts per week (Thu, Fri, Sat 7 PM ET). From week 5 (Nov 30): 5 per week (Tue to Sat 7 PM ET). 7 per week only when the rules below allow it.
- Long-form test: one 5 to 8 minute original deep dive every two weeks from weeks 9 to 13 (owner approval needed; adds production work).

## Revenue plan (owner target set 2026-11-06: $6,000 per week)
Honest math at 2026 rates (sources in the chat of 2026-11-06): YouTube Shorts ads about $0.33 per 1,000 US views (about 18M US views a week for $6K); long-form education median RPM about $10 (about 600K long-form views a week); TikTok Creator Rewards $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views but only for videos over 60 s and only after 10K followers; sponsors about $10 to $25 per 1,000 views once an audience exists. Channel today: about 2K views a day, not monetized. From ads alone $6K a week is a 12 to 24 month goal that needs a breakout.
Revenue stack, in the order it can start paying:
1. **Production service (fastest path, owner decision pending):** sell Blue and Orange style explainers or a sponsored "explain your product" series to brands; 3 to 4 a week at $1,500 to $2,000 reaches $6K. The channel is the portfolio.
2. **Sponsors and affiliate:** start outreach once Shorts hold 100K+ views a week with US in the top 3 countries.
3. **Long-form ads:** the weekly 12-minute episode is the main ad earner (about 30x Shorts per view); Shorts feed it via the Related video link.
4. **Shorts ads:** after YPP (1K subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days, stricter from Feb 1, 2027).
5. **TikTok Creator Rewards:** add 61 s+ TikTok cuts once TikTok passes 5K followers (rewards need 10K followers and 60 s+ videos).
Every weekly review reports dollars by source against the $6K target.

## Growth playbook (researched 2026-11-03, ranked by impact for this channel)
Built from 2026 sources (Metricool 2026 YouTube Study, OverseerOS per-day study, vidIQ, 1of10, YouTube policy pages). Creator-reported benchmarks are not official YouTube numbers; we judge by our own medians.

| # | Lever | Why | What we do | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cross-post every Short to TikTok and Instagram Reels** | Same clean 9:16 file runs 3 independent algorithms; Metricool measured far higher average views per short video on TikTok and Reels than on Shorts | Metricool post with providers youtube + tiktok + instagram (REEL), one clean master, no watermark, per-platform caption | Agent (automatic) |
| 2 | **Win the first 1 to 2 seconds** (viewed vs swiped away 70%+) | Shorts are tested on a small seed audience first; swipe-away is the first gate | Frame 1 shows the object mid-action or the payoff preview plus the hook subtitle; no greeting, no slow pan, first line under 9 words | Agent |

| 6 | **Search packaging** | Shorts now have a Search filter; titles matter | Title 30 to 50 characters, the searched phrase first ("Why does my toilet keep running?"), keyword in description line 1 | Agent |
| 7 | **Hype from early fans** | Hype gives small channels (under 500K) a temporary boost in the first 7 days | Owner asks friends and family to Hype each new Short | Owner |
| 8 | **Ride trends (freshness bias)** | Recent uploads get favored | Trend-led queue, scout 4x per day (DEMAND.md, TRENDS.md) | Agent |
| 9 | **Double down on outliers** | One breakout Short can move the whole channel | Any Short at 5x the trailing median: 2 to 3 follow-ups within 7 days, same hook shape | Agent |
| 10 | **Auto-dubbing** | YouTube auto-dubbing (27 languages) adds non-English recommendation pools | Turn it on in Studio settings once eligible; test on the top Shorts first | Owner, one-time |

**Guardrails (do not trade these for speed):**
- Volume vs quality: more Shorts per day raise total views, but per-Short views tend to drop. During the 4-per-day sprint, if viewed-vs-swiped or average percentage viewed falls more than 10% below the channel's prior median for 3 days, cut to 2 per day (weekly review applies this).
- Inauthentic content policy: YouTube does not monetize mass-produced or template-identical content. Keep the variation rule, real research per episode, and distinct gags; never auto-generate near-duplicates.
- Never: bought views or subs, sub-for-sub, engagement pods, watermarked re-uploads, misleading titles. They risk the channel and break the algorithm's audience model.

## Decision rules
- Above the optimistic row 2 weeks in a row: re-forecast up, move to 7 per week, start sponsor outreach.
- Between conservative and base: hold the plan, fix only the weakest metric.
- Below the conservative row 3 weeks in a row: stop scaling, run a 2-week topic test (half the slots on the best title shape, half on a new one, keep the winner).
- Any Short at 5x the trailing median: 2 or 3 follow-ups on the same pattern within 7 days, linked as related videos.
- Any Short under 60% viewed: rewrite that hook template in RUNBOOK.md.
- Raise cadence only if viewed vs swiped stays within 5 points of its prior 3-week average.
- Any spam or inauthentic-content warning: freeze cadence, increase template variation.
- Topic choice follows the demand desk (DEMAND.md): BUY signals first, portfolio about 60% evergreen, 30% seasonal, 10% trend. The weekly review adds the P&L by lane (7-day views divided by the trailing median).
- Growth hack quota: at least 1 trend or calendar episode per week (TRENDS.md). Review its 7-day views vs the trailing median; if trend episodes beat the median 2 weeks running, allow 2 per week.
- Week 13: re-baseline this whole table with the channel's own numbers.
