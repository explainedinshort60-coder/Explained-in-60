# Growth plan and weekly review

Source: research report "Explained in 60 growth plan" (Nov 2026). All targets are derived estimates; re-baseline at week 13 with the channel's own median.

## Key facts (reported)
- Full YPP for new applicants from Feb 1, 2027: 1,000 subs + 20M Shorts views in 90 days, or 8,000 long-form watch hours in 365 days. Shorts pool revenue only while holding 10M Shorts views per rolling 90 days.
- Lower tier (fan funding, no ad revenue): 500 subs + 3 uploads + 3M Shorts views in 90 days.
- Median US Shorts RPM about $0.33 per 1,000 views. Early money = sponsorships at about $10 to $25 per 1,000 views, plus affiliate.
- New-channel median: about 1,300 views per Short in 30 days; 20.9% of Shorts pass 10K; 48% of channels get a 10K Short within their first 10.
- Viewed vs swiped: under 60% performs poorly, 70 to 90% performs well.

## Cadence
- Weeks 1 to 4: 3 Shorts per week (Thu, Fri, Sat 7 PM ET). From week 5 (Nov 30): 5 per week (Tue to Sat 7 PM ET). 7 per week only when the rules below allow it.
- Long-form test: one 5 to 8 minute original deep dive every two weeks from weeks 9 to 13 (owner approval needed; adds production work).

## Decision rules
- Above the optimistic row 2 weeks in a row: re-forecast up, move to 7 per week, start sponsor outreach.
- Between conservative and base: hold the plan, fix only the weakest metric.
- Below the conservative row 3 weeks in a row: stop scaling, run a 2-week topic test (half the slots on the best title shape, half on a new one, keep the winner).
- Any Short at 5x the trailing median: 2 or 3 follow-ups on the same pattern within 7 days, linked as related videos.
- Any Short under 60% viewed: rewrite that hook template in RUNBOOK.md.
- Raise cadence only if viewed vs swiped stays within 5 points of its prior 3-week average.
- Any spam or inauthentic-content warning: freeze cadence, increase template variation.
- Growth hack quota: at least 1 trend or calendar episode per week (TRENDS.md). Review its 7-day views vs the trailing median; if trend episodes beat the median 2 weeks running, allow 2 per week.
- Week 13: re-baseline this whole table with the channel's own numbers.
