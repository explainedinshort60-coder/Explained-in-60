# 012 QB helmet: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 012  /  "HOW DOES THE QB HEAR THE PLAY IN HIS HELMET?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). Frame 1: the host (the coach, in a headset) mid-call, pointing at a helmet on a stand; radio
        pulses fly from his mic into the ear hole, the speaker buzzes, a bubble pops out of the helmet: SLANT LEFT!
        Play clock on the post ticking 31, 30, 29. Stadium crowd.
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Who's talking inside a quarterback's helmet?

03.00  TURN (3-13 s). Close on the host: HEADSET. Mic close: MIC, pulses start. Wide: the pulses ride to the helmet (RADIO).
        NARRATOR (3.0s): His coach. Live, mid-game.
        NARRATOR (6.25s): The coach talks into this headset,
        NARRATOR (10.0s): and a radio signal zips it onto the field.

13.25  REVEAL (13.25-19.75 s). X-ray helmet: padding, SPEAKER at the ear hole, RECEIVER and wire (ding). Wide: ONE-WAY.
        NARRATOR (13.25s): Tucked by his ear: a tiny speaker.
        NARRATOR (16.5s): Here's the kicker: it only goes one way.

19.75  ONE-WAY (19.75-22.75 s). The helmet tries to answer (bubble COACH??), a red dashed reply fizzles into an X. Pop: NOPE. (boing)
        NARRATOR (19.75s): He can't answer back. Coach talks, he listens.

22.75  THE TIMER + GAG (22.75-36.25 s). Play clock pops in at 40 (PLAY CLOCK). Wide: it races down (ratchet) while the
        pulses fly. At 15 the digits go red, record scratch, zap: the signal breaks into a red zigzag and an X, the
        antenna light turns red. Pop: BZZT! The host keeps yelling into a dead mic, then flails his arms like a
        semaphore. Pop: PLAN B! The helmet bubble: HUH? (slide whistle, boing, rimshot)
        NARRATOR (22.75s): And there's a timer on it.
        NARRATOR (25.5s): The play clock counts down from 40,
        NARRATOR (29.5s): and at 15 seconds, the radio cuts out.
        NARRATOR (32.9s): Mid-sentence? Too bad. Coach is on mute.

36.25  GREEN DOT (36.25-43 s). Close on the back of the helmet: the green dot pulses (GREEN DOT, ding). Cut to the line of
        scrimmage: 3 offense helmets facing 3 defense helmets; pulses from a sideline headset reach only the one
        dotted helmet on each side (OFFENSE, DEFENSE, crowd ooh).
        NARRATOR (36.25s): So who gets the speaker? Find the green dot.
        NARRATOR (39.6s): One player on offense, one on defense.

43.00  BONUS (43-50 s). Push in on the offense dot (QB, badge 1994), then the defense dot (DEFENSE, badge 2008).
        NARRATOR (42.9s): Bonus: quarterbacks got radios in 1994,
        NARRATOR (46.25s): and defenses had to wait until 2008.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). Back to the opening composition, x-ray on (SPEAKER, HEADSET), the clock counting from 40.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So that voice in his helmet is the coach...
        NARRATOR (53.5s): One-way, on a timer. Lowkey genius.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). X-ray fades, the bubble SLANT LEFT! returns, clock lands on 31: back to frame 0.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```
