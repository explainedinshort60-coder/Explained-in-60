# 005 Escalator handrail: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 005  /  "WHERE DOES THE ESCALATOR HANDRAIL GO?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). WS. Host at the bottom of an up escalator, hand resting on the moving coral handrail, steps rolling.
        Camera tracks up the rail to the top end, where it curls over and vanishes. Pop: THE END?
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Where does the escalator handrail... go?

03.00  TURN (3-10 s). Cut to the FULL side view. X-RAY: the balustrade goes see-through, a light dash races around the
        whole rail: it is a closed loop. ONE LOOP. Push in on the middle: HAND on the rail, FEET on the steps.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): It never ends. It's one rubber loop.
        NARRATOR (6.25s): But something has to match it to your feet.

10.00  MECHANISM + REVEALS (10-29 s). One part per beat.
        Track up the TOP RUN.                               NARRATOR (10.0s): Your hand rides the top run.
        Top end, x-ray: ROLLERS inside the curl.            NARRATOR (12.75s): Rollers swing it around the end,
        Camera rides down the hidden RETURN run.            NARRATOR (15.25s): it sneaks back underneath, upside down,
        Bottom: DRIVE WHEEL under the return run.           NARRATOR (18.5s): and down here, a drive wheel grabs it.
        Tight: two spring rollers SQUEEZE (clunk).          NARRATOR (21.75s): Rollers squeeze it tight, and friction pushes it along.
        Tilt into the pit: MOTOR, sprocket, CHAIN up to the wheel.
                                                            NARRATOR (25.25s): That wheel is chained to the same motor as the steps.

29.00  GAG (29-38 s). MS. Host presents, then plops a rubber duck on the handrail (pop). 4X: the camera chases the duck
        up the rail (slide whistle up), over the top (boing), back down the hidden return UPSIDE DOWN (slide whistle down),
        through the drive wheel, around the bottom curl, and it lands right back at the host's hand. Host recoils.
        Pop: WHEEE!, then IT'S BACK!
        NARRATOR (29.0s): Hand and feet, same ride.
        NARRATOR (31.25s): Don't believe me? Send in the duck.
        NARRATOR (33.75s): Lowkey the best ride in the mall.
        NARRATOR (36.25s): And it's back. Every time.

38.30  DEMO (38.3-41.7 s). FULL x-ray, normal speed, the light dash laps the loop.
        NARRATOR (38.3s): Up, around, under, through the wheel. Forever.

41.70  BONUS (41.7-50 s). Camera rides up the incline with a HAND icon on the rail and a FOOT on a step, joined by a
        dashed line. The foot creeps ahead, the line tilts. Record scratch. Pop: SLIP!
        NARRATOR (41.7s): Here's the kicker: hand drifting behind your feet?
        NARRATOR (45.4s): Worn rubber stretches, and the grip slips a little.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). WS, same as the opening. Host proud, then explains.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So the handrail never ends.
        NARRATOR (53.5s): It just loops back around, all day.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). Host's hand back on the rail, flows into the hook.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```

Words: 128 spoken. Slang: "lowkey", "here's the kicker" (2).

## Craft rule (no negation list) and replacement line
- No "No X. No Y. No Z." anywhere (VO, captions, description).
- Replacement positive stakes line, shown: "But something has to match it to your feet." over HAND and FEET callouts, paid off by the duck lap and the SLIP bonus. First moving parts (rail texture and steps) are moving at frame 0; the loop is revealed at 3.5 s.

## Variation vs episodes 002, 003, 004 (need 3 of 6)
- Camera path: long diagonal tracking shots (up the top run, down the hidden return, a chase cam on the duck, a ride-along up the incline), plus a pit tilt (004: dive into a button and cuts to a top-view card; 003: vertical tilt). CHANGED
- Host blocking: hand resting on a moving rail, then launching a prop that leaves and comes back to him (004: works a door and taps a button). CHANGED
- Prop staging: a two-floor architectural cutaway (diagonal escalator, mezzanine, machine pit) instead of a single appliance or object (004: fridge plus a separate card). CHANGED
- Gag type: round-trip chase gag (a rubber duck rides the whole loop, upside down on the way back, and boomerangs into the host's hand) (004: rhythm strobe "fridge rave"). CHANGED
- Color accent: coral handrail and a yellow duck (004: warm yellow light on navy). CHANGED
- Hook shape: "Where does X go?" location mystery, answered with a reveal of the hidden half (004: yes/no doubt question). CHANGED

## Better than 004
- Tighter hook: the camera physically follows the question (rides the rail to the spot where it vanishes) and the loop answer lands at 3.5 s.
- More visual change: 6 distinct camera moves in the first 29 s (track up, x-ray full, track, top end, ride down the return, drive, squeeze push-in, pit tilt), moving rail texture, steps, sprocket, chain and drive wheel in every frame.
- Funnier gag: a character-like prop (duck) with a payoff structure (leaves, flips upside down, comes back and bonks the host), scored with slide whistles and a boing, instead of a strobe.
- Useful bonus with a fail state the viewer has felt (hand drifting behind the feet) shown live with a tilting line.
