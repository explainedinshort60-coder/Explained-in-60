## Claims and sources
| Claim in the script | Source |
|---|---|
| Thieves take cards right off the store rack / display | FTC, "Check out gift cards before you buy them" (2024-12-19): "Scammers sometimes tamper with gift cards in store displays." https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/12/check-out-gift-cards-you-buy-them ; DC Attorney General alert (2025-12-15): "Scammers may take an unpurchased gift card off the rack" https://oag.dc.gov/release/attorney-general-brian-schwalb-issues-alert |
| They scratch or peel the back and copy the number and PIN | FTC: "They might remove the protective stickers from gift cards or even scratch off the back. That way, they can steal the card numbers and PIN." DC AG: "scratch off the back of the card to reveal the code, and then cover it up again." |
| Then they cover it up and hang it back (it sits there like a trap) | DC AG: "and then cover it up again"; "scan the codes of unpurchased gift cards off the rack and wait until you buy and activate the gift card". |
| You pay, the card goes live; they wait, once money lands they spend it online, without the card | FTC: "a scammer can get any money you put on a gift card, even if they don't physically have the card. They just wait for you to load money on the card and, poof, it's gone." DC AG: "Once the card is bought and activated, the scammer accesses the card online (using the swiped code) and steals the money on the card." |
| Some swap the barcode, so your money loads their card | DC AG: "replace the barcode with a barcode that has already been activated. After the card is bought and activated, its funds are diverted to the scammer's card with the alternate barcode." |
| Check the back: stickers on, PIN covered, nothing scratched | FTC, "Avoiding and Reporting Gift Card Scams": "Make sure the protective stickers are on the card and that it doesn't look like someone tampered with them. Also check that the PIN number on the back isn't showing." https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/avoiding-and-reporting-gift-card-scams ; DC AG: check "if anyone has scratched off any information or added or removed any stickers". |
| Looks messed with? Grab another one and tell the cashier | FTC article: "Pick a different gift card if you spot a problem and show the tampered card to a cashier or manager." FTC alert: "tell a cashier or manager". |
| Keep the receipt and a photo of the card | FTC article: "Always keep a copy of the gift card and store receipt. Take a picture of the gift card and store receipt with your phone." |
| Anyone asking you to pay in gift cards: scam | FTC article: "Only scammers will tell you to buy a gift card ... and give them the numbers off the back of the card. No matter what they say, that's a scam." "No real business or government agency will ever tell you to buy a gift card to pay them." FTC alert 2023-12: "Only scammers say that." https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/12/keep-your-money-safe-gift-card-scammers-holiday-season-heres-how |
