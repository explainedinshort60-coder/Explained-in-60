# 013 Gift card draining podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 013  /  "HOW DO SCAMMERS DRAIN GIFT CARDS?"  /  PODCAST  /  SERIES: WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES

(COLD OPEN: a gift card at the register, BALANCE $0.00, dollar bills flying out of it, hook caption)
BLUE    [offended] I just loaded this gift card. It's empty!
ORANGE  [deadpan] Somebody spent it. Before you even bought it.             (tiny push)
BLUE    [sarcastic] Cool. A time-traveling thief.
ORANGE  [sighs] Thieves grab cards right off the store rack.                 (INS: rack, gloved hand lifts a card)
ORANGE  They scratch or peel the back and copy the number and PIN.           (INS: card back, sticker peels, notepad copies)
ORANGE  Then they cover it up and hang it back.                              (INS: sticker back on, card back on the rack)
BLUE    [curious] So it just sits there? Like a trap?
ORANGE  [deadpan] A trap with a bow on it.
ORANGE  You pay at the register. The card goes live.                         (INS: register, +$50, CARD GOES LIVE)
ORANGE  They wait. Once money lands, they spend it online.                   (INS: money runs to the laptop, $0.00, POOF)
BLUE    [gasps] They don't even need the card?
ORANGE  Nope. Some even swap the barcode, so your money loads their card.    (INS: fake barcode slides on, $50 bends to THEIRS)
BLUE    [laughs] So I bought a gift card. For a criminal.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Very generous. He says thanks.                             (push-in, the one crack)
BLUE    [suspicious] So how do I not get robbed?
ORANGE  Check the back. Stickers on, PIN covered, nothing scratched.         (INS: magnifier, three ticks)
ORANGE  Looks messed with? Grab another one and tell the cashier.
ORANGE  Keep the receipt and a photo of the card.                            (INS: receipt + phone photo, KEEP BOTH)
ORANGE  And anyone asking you to pay in gift cards? Scam.                    (INS: text PAY ME IN GIFT CARDS, SCAM stamp)
BLUE    [whispering] Trust no rack.
ORANGE  [sighs] Sure. And now you know.
BLUE    [curious] Anyway. So yesterday...   (loops into the hook)
```
