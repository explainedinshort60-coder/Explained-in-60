# 001 toilet: podcast remake script (as edited)

```
BLUE    (suspicious) How does my toilet know when to STOP filling?
ORANGE  (deadpan) It doesn't know anything. It's a toilet.
BLUE    (whispering) Then who's watching it?
ORANGE  (flatly) A floating ball.
BLUE    (laughs) A ball? Like a pool toy?
ORANGE  Basically. It floats on the water, on the end of an arm.
ORANGE  You flush, the water drops, and the ball drops with it.
ORANGE  The arm tips down and opens a little valve. Water rushes in.
BLUE    (excited) And the ball rides the water back up!
ORANGE  Yep. Higher water, higher ball. The arm lifts... and pushes the valve shut.
ORANGE  Water stops at the exact same line. Every single time.
BLUE    (amazed) No sensor? No chip?
BLUE    (gasps) So my toilet is smarter than my smart fridge.
ORANGE  (chuckles) Your fridge has an app. Your toilet has a ball.
BLUE    (suspicious) Then why does mine keep running at night?
ORANGE  Ball set too high, or a worn valve. The extra water sneaks down the overflow pipe.
BLUE    (delighted) So my toilet's just... thirsty. (giggles)
ORANGE  (sighs) Sure. And now you know.
BLUE    (curious) Cool. But for real...
```
Cut in the edit: Orange's "No sensor. No chip. No wifi." (negation triplet rule, 2026-11-02).

But for real..." vs "Cool. But real question..."). Same object family as flush v3 (toilet) but a different mechanism (fill valve vs flush siphon).
