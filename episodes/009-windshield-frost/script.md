# 009 Windshield frost podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 009  /  "WHY IS MY WINDSHIELD FROZEN WHEN IT'S 37?"  /  PODCAST

(COLD OPEN: frost spreading across the windshield, air thermometer 37°F, hook caption)
BLUE    [offended] It's 37 out. Why is my windshield frozen?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Because your windshield isn't 37.                                     (tiny push)
BLUE    [sarcastic] Wow. Thank you, professor.
ORANGE  [sighs] That 37 is the air, measured about four feet up.                        (INS: thermometer, 4 FT)
ORANGE  Your glass is lying back, staring straight up at the night sky.                  (INS: glass, tilt up to sky)
BLUE    [curious] Stargazing? That's kind of romantic.
ORANGE  [deadpan] It's losing heat. Everything glows invisible heat,
        and a clear sky sends back barely any.                                           (INS: heat arrows up, tiny return)
ORANGE  So the glass drops below freezing while the air stays above it.                  (INS: dial 37 to 30°F)
BLUE    [gasps] My car is colder than the weather?
ORANGE  Yep. Then water vapor touches the glass and turns straight into ice.             (INS: vapor lands, ICE!)
BLUE    [laughs] So my car stared at space all night and froze. Relatable.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Your car has a better night routine than you.                          (push-in, the one crack)
BLUE    [suspicious] Then why is my neighbor's car totally clear?
ORANGE  Carport. That roof is warmer than the sky, so it sends heat back down.           (INS: carport, glass clears)
ORANGE  Clouds do the same thing. A blanket for your car.                                (INS: clouds)
BLUE    [whispering] So I park under his carport. Got it.
ORANGE  [sighs] Or a tree. And now you know.                                             (INS: tree)
BLUE    [offended] Still. Make it make sense...   (loops into the hook)
```

18 lines, 162 words. Slang: "Relatable", "Make it make sense" (2). No negation triplet. No line starts with "Okay". No em dashes.
