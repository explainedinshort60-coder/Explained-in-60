# 003 Zipper: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 003  /  "HOW DOES A ZIPPER WORK?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). WS. Host yanks the jacket panel three times. Fabric stretches, stand wobbles, zipper holds.
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Try ripping a zipper apart... nope.

03.00  TURN (3-6 s). Host grabs the pull tab and bends: ZIP! The top springs open in a V.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): But this little slider? One second.

06.00  MECHANISM (6-12 s). MS, x-ray fades in. Callouts: TEETH, SLIDER, Y CHANNEL.
        NARRATOR (6.3s): Two rows of teeth, staggered,
        NARRATOR (8.3s): plus a slider
        NARRATOR (10.2s): with a Y-shaped tunnel inside.

12.00  REVEALS (12-27 s). CU, slow zip up, camera tilts with the slider. LEFT / RIGHT teeth light as they exit.
        Pan left: magnifier with the side profile, teeth drop in one by one. BUMP, DENT, LOCKED.
        NARRATOR (12.0s): Zip up, and the Y squeezes both rows together,
        NARRATOR (15.4s): one tooth from the left, one from the right.
        NARRATOR (18.8s): Each tooth has a bump on top and a dent underneath.
        NARRATOR (22.7s): Each bump drops into the next dent. Locked.

26.70  GAG (27-33 s). WS. Host tries again, harder: three yanks, leans way back, stand wobbles. NOPE. Grip slips, host recoils.
        NARRATOR (26.7s): So pulling sideways does... nothing.
        NARRATOR (29.2s): Lowkey the strongest grip in your closet.

33.00  DEMO + BONUS (33-50 s). CU zip down (SPLIT), MS up and down (LOCKED, OPEN).
        Worn slider: zipped up but the teeth below split open. Host facepalms. Pliers slide in, squeeze, zip down and up: LOCKED.
        NARRATOR (33.0s): Zip down, and the Y splits them apart again.
        NARRATOR (36.3s): Up: locked. Down: open. Every time.
        NARRATOR (40.0s): Here's the kicker. If yours keeps splitting,
        NARRATOR (43.8s): the slider's just worn loose.
        NARRATOR (46.0s): Pinch it a hair tighter, with pliers. Fixed.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). WS, same as the opening. Host back to the panel edge.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): No glue. No magnets.
        NARRATOR (53.5s): Just tiny bumps in tiny dents.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). Host starts the first yank, flows into the hook.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```

Words: 113 spoken. Slang: "lowkey", "here's the kicker" (2).

## Variation vs episodes 001 and 002 (need 3 of 6)
- Camera path: vertical tilt that follows the slider, plus a lateral pan to a magnifier inset (002: static push-ins on a fixed pen). CHANGED
- Host blocking: host yanks the prop (tug with lean back), bends to pull the tab (002: hand parked on the button). CHANGED
- Prop staging: tall jacket on a coat stand, mechanism on the outside face with an x-ray slider (001 tank cutaway, 002 small pen on a stand). CHANGED
- Gag type: physical strain fail (yank, NOPE, grip slips) (002: CLICK pop and recoil). CHANGED
- Color accent: large cobalt fabric field with ivory teeth and a pale lining (002: cobalt cam inside ivory pen). CHANGED
- Hook shape: dare / challenge statement ("Try ripping a zipper apart... nope.") (002: "Why..." question). CHANGED

## Better than 002
- Hook shows the phenomenon on frame 1 (the yank) and the payoff by 4 s (ZIP!).
- Visual change every 1 to 3 s: tug, zip, x-ray, tilt, lit teeth, magnifier, gag, split, pliers.
- Funnier gag: escalation in three yanks, stand wobble, NOPE, then the grip slips.
- Useful bonus (the pliers fix) gives a reason to share and save.
