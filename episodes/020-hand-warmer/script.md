# 020 Hand warmer: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 020  /  "HOW DO HAND WARMERS GET HOT?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). Frame 1: wide. The host holds an open hand warmer packet, glowing orange, heat waves rising,
        badge 135°F, RUST?! pops. Host grinning. Deep whoosh.
        NARRATOR (0.0s): This little packet is rusting in your hand.

03.00  INSIDE (3-9.5 s). Close: an x-ray magnifier into the pouch. Gray iron grains (IRON POWDER), then salt cubes
        (SALT), charcoal bits (CHARCOAL), damp filler flakes with water drops (DAMP FILLER), one callout at a time.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): Inside: a pile of super fine iron powder,
        NARRATOR (6.25s): plus salt, charcoal and a damp filler.

09.50  WRAPPER (9.5-12.5 s). Wide: the packet sealed in its clear wrapper, frosty and cold, no glow.
        NARRATOR (9.5s): Here's the kicker: the wrapper keeps air out.

12.50  TEAR (12.5-16 s). The tear strip flies off, the wrapper slides away. Close x-ray: oxygen molecules drop in
        through the pores in the fabric (TINY HOLES).
        NARRATOR (12.5s): Tear it open, and air slips in through tiny holes.

16.00  RUST (16-19.5 s). Oxygen lands on iron grains, each one flashes and turns rust orange (RUST).
        NARRATOR (16.0s): Oxygen grabs the iron and turns it into rust.

19.50  HEAT (19.5-23.5 s). Wide: the packet glows, heat waves, badge climbs 70°F to 135°F, slide whistle up.
        NARRATOR (19.5s): Rusting gives off heat, and salt makes it rust crazy fast.

23.50  GAG (23.5-27.5 s). The host leans over a rusty old nail on a crate, warming his hands over it (RUSTY NAIL).
        Nothing. Cold wind, record scratch, sad trombone, BRRR., he shivers and facepalms.
        NARRATOR (23.5s): This old nail did the same thing. Just way too slow.

27.50  CHARCOAL (27.5-31 s). Close x-ray: heat rings ripple out from the charcoal bits (CHARCOAL).
        NARRATOR (27.5s): Charcoal spreads the heat around,

31.00  TOASTY (31-35 s). Wide: even glow, badge 135°F, ding.
        NARRATOR (31.0s): so it stays toasty, up to about 135 degrees.

35.00  SHAKE (35-38.5 s). The host shakes the packet, the glow surges, HOTTER!
        NARRATOR (35.0s): Shake it, and more air gets in. Hotter, faster.

38.50  BONUS (38.5-46 s). A zip bag closes around it, the glow fades, PAUSED. Then the bag opens, glow back, BACK ON!
        NARRATOR (38.5s): Bonus: zip it in an airtight bag, and it stops.
        NARRATOR (42.0s): Open the bag later, and it fires right back up.

46.00  DONE (46-50 s). Close x-ray: every iron grain is rust, badge DONE. Wide: cold, used-up packet, host frustrated.
        NARRATOR (46.0s): After 7 to 24 hours, the iron is all rust. Done.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). Back to the opening composition: fresh packet glowing, 135°F, host grinning.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So that warm packet is rust on fast-forward.
        NARRATOR (53.75s): Wild, right?

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). RUST?! pops back in at the frame 0 position.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```
