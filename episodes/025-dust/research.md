Myth (Blue): "Dust is basically all dead skin." The VO says skin is part of it, most of it comes from outside, and "one study found over sixty percent", never a universal number.
Not used: dust mite claims, lead or arsenic in dust (health scare, not needed), exact share of skin, pet dander health claims.
Gross content mild: no flakes close-up of skin, just pale peach chips in a magnifier; Orange's "You're both gross" is a joke about shoes.
