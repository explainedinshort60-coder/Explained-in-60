# 024 How To Open Any Stuck Jar Lid (podcast, You've Been Doing It Wrong)

ORANGE  There's a vacuum inside. Less air in the jar than out here.
ORANGE  So all the air outside is pressing the lid down onto the glass.     [AIR PUSH arrows: the OH!]
BLUE    (gasps) The sky is in on it?!
ORANGE  That push jams the seal tight, so twisting fights a lot of friction.
ORANGE  So let the air in. Slide a spoon under the edge of the lid and lift gently.
ORANGE  When it hisses, the seal is broken.
BLUE    (whispering) It hissed at me.
ORANGE  (dry) Still stuck? Run hot tap water on the metal lid. Metal grows more than glass when it heats up.
ORANGE  The lid loosens just a hair. Then twist.
BLUE    (laughs) So I lost a fight to the sky.                              [Orange's one crack, as listener]
ORANGE  (sighs) Every time. And now you know.
BLUE    (straining) I am still squeezing.                                   [loops to the stuck jar]
