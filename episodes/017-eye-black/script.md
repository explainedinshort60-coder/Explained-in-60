# 017 Eye black podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 017  /  "DOES EYE BLACK ACTUALLY WORK?"  /  PODCAST  /  SPRINT (no series badge; filed as Is It Really?)

(COLD OPEN: game day, a blazing sun, the ball dropping out of the glare, a player with eye black squinting, red "?"; hook caption)
BLUE    [curious] Does eye black actually do anything?
ORANGE  [deadpan] It makes you look like a raccoon.                           (tiny push)
BLUE    [offended] It's war paint! It's supposed to look scary!
ORANGE  [sighs] The idea is, sunlight hits your cheekbones...                 (INS: side profile, a sun ray travels down to the cheekbone)
ORANGE  and bounces right up into your eyes. That's glare.                    (INS: it bounces into the eye, flare, squint, GLARE)
ORANGE  Glare washes out the ball against a bright sky.                       (Blue listens, then INS: the player's view, ball fades next to the sun)
ORANGE  Black grease soaks up that light instead of bouncing it.              (INS: grease paints on, the ray is soaked up, check)
BLUE    [gasps] So it's sunglasses... for your face?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Tiny, greasy sunglasses. Sure.                              (push)
ORANGE  In 2003, Yale doctors tested it in bright sunlight.                   (INS: 2003 YALE TEST eye charts, rows fade)
ORANGE  With the grease, people saw contrast a little better.                 (INS: one more row on the GREASE chart, +1 ROW)
BLUE    [smug] Told you. Science says I'm scary.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Science says you're slightly less blinded.                  (push-in, the one crack)
ORANGE  And those stickers with the logos? They didn't help at all.           (INS: a star sticker slaps on, the ray still bounces, X)
BLUE    [gasps] My stickers are a lie?                                        (the one shock face)
ORANGE  [deadpan] They scored the same as a smear of petroleum jelly.         (INS: scoreboard GREASE BETTER, STICKERS SAME, PETROLEUM JELLY SAME)
BLUE    [laughs] Grape jelly on my face. Game day.                            (two-shot laugh, then INS: grape jelly smear and jar)
ORANGE  [deadpan] Wrong jelly.                                                (INS: X, WRONG JELLY)
ORANGE  Players used to smear on burnt cork. The grease is the upgrade.       (INS: burnt cork rubs a black smudge, BURNT CORK)
ORANGE  [sighs] It works. A little. And now you know.
BLUE    [whispering] Still looks scary though. So real question...            (loops into the hook)
```
