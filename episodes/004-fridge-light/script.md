## Craft rule (no negation list) and replacement line
- No "No X. No Y. No Z." anywhere (VO, captions, description).
- Replacement positive stakes line, shown: "You've shut it a thousand times. Watch this button." while the camera dives into the button (the "you've done this a thousand times" moment). First moving part on screen at frame 0 (the door), the switch at 4 s, the plunger moving at 18 s.
