# 015 Touchscreen gloves podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 015  /  "WHY DON'T TOUCHSCREENS WORK WITH GLOVES?"  /  PODCAST  /  SPRINT (no series badge)

(COLD OPEN: night bus stop, snow, a phone ringing MOM, a red knit glove jabbing the green answer button, nothing; hook caption)
BLUE    [annoyed] Why does my phone ignore me in gloves?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Because it can't feel you. At all.                         (tiny push)
BLUE    [offended] Rude. I'm right here!
ORANGE  [sighs] Under the glass is a grid of tiny invisible wires.           (INS: x-ray, the wire grid appears)
ORANGE  They hold a little electric charge, all the time.                    (INS: yellow charge sparks along the grid)
ORANGE  Your skin conducts. So when you touch, some charge flows into your finger.   (Blue listens, then INS: section, charge hops up into the finger)
ORANGE  The grid feels that dip, right there. That's your tap.               (INS: bare finger lands, dip ring, TAP!)
BLUE    [curious] So my finger steals electricity?
ORANGE  [deadpan] A tiny bit. Yes. Every tap.
ORANGE  Wool and leather don't conduct. The charge has nowhere to go.        (INS: section, knit glove, charge bounces back, X)
ORANGE  So to the screen, you're just not there.                             (Blue listens, then INS: glove hand goes ghost, X)
BLUE    [gasps] I'm a ghost!
ORANGE  [deadpan] A ghost with very warm hands.                              (push-in)
BLUE    [curious] Wait. Would a sausage work?
ORANGE  [sighs] Yep. It's salty and wet, so it conducts. People really tried it.   (INS: sausage in a gloved fist, dip, TAP!)
BLUE    [laughs] I'm bringing a hot dog to the bus stop.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Please don't.                                              (push-in, the one crack)
ORANGE  Get touchscreen gloves. The fingertips have conductive thread sewn in.     (INS: glove close-up, silver thread tips)
ORANGE  The charge runs through the thread into you, and the tap lands.      (INS: section, charge runs up the thread, check)
BLUE    [whispering] Or I just use my nose.
ORANGE  [sighs] Sure. And now you know.
BLUE    [shivering] Ugh. It's so cold out, and...   (loops into the hook)
```
