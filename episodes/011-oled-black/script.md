# 011 OLED perfect black podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 011  /  "WHY DO OLED SCREENS SHOW PERFECT BLACK?"  /  PODCAST

(COLD OPEN: dim room, LCD TV black glowing gray behind a true-black OLED tablet, same night-sky picture, hook caption)
BLUE    [curious] Why is this black blacker than my TV?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Because it's not showing black. It's showing nothing.        (tiny push)
BLUE    [sarcastic] Deep. Put that on a mug.
ORANGE  [sighs] Every dot on that new tablet is its own tiny lamp.             (INS: pixel grid lights up, TINY LAMP)
ORANGE  The O is for organic. A thin layer that glows when powered.            (INS: pixel cross-section, current flows, ORGANIC LAYER)
BLUE    [curious] So my screen is a stadium full of phone flashlights?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Sure. And for black, those lamps just switch off.            (INS: dark lamps switch off, OFF = BLACK)
BLUE    [gasps] Hold up. Black is just the lights being off?
ORANGE  Yep. A regular LCD TV can't do that.                                    (INS: the TV)
ORANGE  One big light sits behind the whole screen, always on.                  (INS: x-ray, ONE BIG LIGHT)
ORANGE  Liquid crystals twist like tiny blinds to block it.                     (Blue listens, then INS: LCD stack, blinds twist shut)
ORANGE  But blinds leak. So your black is really dark gray.                     (INS: leak, DARK GRAY)
BLUE    [laughs] So my TV's been gaslighting me in gray.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Your TV is a night light with ambition.                       (push-in, the one crack)
BLUE    [suspicious] So what's the catch with the lamp one?
ORANGE  Leave one image up for ages and those lamps wear unevenly.              (INS: static bar, clock spins)
ORANGE  That's burn-in. A faint ghost that sticks around.                       (INS: gray screen, ghost bar, BURN-IN)
BLUE    [gasps] A haunted screen? Sick.
ORANGE  [sighs] Not sick. And now you know.
BLUE    [curious] Wait. Then explain this one...   (loops into the hook)
```
