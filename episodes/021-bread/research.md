# Fact sheet: 021 bread (viewer request, @Ducksonic12)
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Baker's yeast (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) eats sugars, makes CO2 and ethanol | Gisslen, Professional Cooking; food-science texts |
| Glutenin + gliadin + water + kneading = gluten, a stretchy net that traps the gas | standard baking science (King Arthur, McGee) |
| Oven spring: trapped gas expands in the oven before the yeast die | Gisslen, Professional Cooking 10th ed. |
| Yeast thermal death point about 138 to 140°F | Gisslen; Bakerpedia "yeast kill" |
| Gluten firms and starch gelatinizes, setting the bubbles | McGee, On Food and Cooking |
| Most ethanol evaporates; trace 0.04 to 1.9% remains; part of the aroma | 1926 ACS report via Mental Floss / ScienceABC |
