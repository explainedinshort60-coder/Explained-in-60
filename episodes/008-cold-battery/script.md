# 008 Cold battery: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 008  /  "WHY DOES YOUR PHONE BATTERY DIE IN THE COLD?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). MS, snow falling. Host holds his phone: 30%. At 0.5 s the bar drains to 0%, glitch, the screen
        goes black at 1.2 s. Host recoils. Pop: WHAT?!
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Thirty percent... and your phone just died?

03.00  TURN (3-10 s). A magnifier bubble pops out of the phone: the battery, full of shivering lithium ions. STILL CHARGED.
        Push in on the bubble.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): Here's the thing: that charge is still in there.
        NARRATOR (6.25s): The cold just jammed the traffic inside your battery.

10.00  MECHANISM, WARM (10-18.5 s). Cut to WARM: tint goes warm, snow stops, phone back on.
        Bubble CU: ELECTRODE, ELECTRODE, ELECTROLYTE.     NARRATOR (10.0s): Inside: two electrodes, with a liquid between them.
        Ions zip across (LITHIUM IONS).                   NARRATOR (12.75s): Lithium ions swim across that liquid.
        WIDE: a power bolt pulses (POWER), host amazed.   NARRATOR (15.5s): Every trip they make powers your phone.

18.50  COLD (18.5-27.75 s). Snow returns, tint goes icy, badge COLD, host shivers.
                                                          NARRATOR (18.5s): Now add the cold.
        Electrolyte turns amber and wavy (SYRUPY).        NARRATOR (21.25s): The liquid thickens up, kind of like syrup.
        ECU: ions get shiver faces and crawl. Pop: BRRR!  NARRATOR (24.5s): And the ions slow to a crawl.

27.75  GAG (27.75-33.3 s). A snail with speed lines slides right past the ions. Pull back: host cracks up.
        Pop: SNAIL WINS! (rimshot, crowd ooh)
        NARRATOR (27.75s): Lowkey, a snail could beat them.
        NARRATOR (31.0s): Sorry, ions.

33.30  DEMO (33.3-40 s). MS: the bolt flickers and fades, 30% drains to 0%, the screen dies, glitch.
        Bubble still full of ions: CHARGE STILL HERE.
        NARRATOR (33.3s): Your phone asks for power. The battery can't keep up.
        NARRATOR (36.7s): So it shuts down, with charge still inside.

40.00  BONUS (40-50 s). Host tucks the phone into his inside pocket (INSIDE POCKET), warm tint. Bubble: syrup clears,
        ions speed up (WARM). Phone comes back out, screen on: 30%, ding. A hair dryer pops in (HAIR DRYER),
        host shakes his head. Pop: NOPE!
        NARRATOR (40.0s): Fix: inside pocket, close to your body.
        NARRATOR (42.9s): The ions warm up and get moving again.
        NARRATOR (45.8s): And your battery comes right back.
        NARRATOR (47.9s): Hair dryer? Nope.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). MS, same as the opening: snow, phone at 30%.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So your battery didn't die. It just got cold.
        NARRATOR (53.5s): Cold liquid. Slow ions. Phone taps out.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). Back to frame 0 (30%, snow), flows into the hook.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```

Words: 120 spoken. Slang: "here's the thing", "lowkey", "taps out" (3).

## Craft rule (no negation list) and replacement line
- No "No X. No Y. No Z." anywhere (VO, captions, description). The takeaway triplet is positive ("Cold liquid. Slow ions. Phone taps out."), not a negation list.
- Replacement positive stakes line, shown: the fail state everyone knows (30% to dead in a second) is the hook itself, then "that charge is still in there" over the bubble full of ions. First moving parts on screen at 0.5 s (battery bar draining) and 3.3 s (shivering ions).

- More visual change: warm/cold tint swaps, snow on/off, bubble open, syrup morph, ions changing speed and faces, power bolt, pocket move, dryer, phone off/on twice.
