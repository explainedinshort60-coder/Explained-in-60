# 007 Flashlight podcast: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 007  /  "WHY DOES A FLASHLIGHT TURN ON WHEN YOU CLICK IT?"  /  PODCAST

BLUE    [curious] What actually happens when I click a flashlight?
ORANGE  [deadpan] Light happens.
BLUE    [offended] I could've said that.
ORANGE  [sighs] Fine. Electricity only flows in a full loop. Out of the battery, and back in.   (INS: x-ray, loop glows)
ORANGE  Batteries sit nose to tail. Plus end on minus end.                                      (INS: + and - tags)
BLUE    [giggles] Like a tiny conga line.                                                       (INS: cells hop)
ORANGE  [flatly] Sure. A spring at the tail keeps them pressed tight together.                  (INS: spring pushes)
ORANGE  And a metal strip runs the length of the tube, back to the switch.                       (INS: strip glows, pan)
BLUE    [curious] So what does my click do?
ORANGE  When it's off, there's a tiny gap. The loop is broken.                                   (INS: GAP)
ORANGE  Click, and a metal piece bridges the gap. Loop closed. Light.                            (INS: disc, LIGHT!)
BLUE    [gasps] And why does it stay on?
ORANGE  A clicky tail has the same guts as a clicky pen. Click on. Click off.                    (INS: latch ON / OFF)
BLUE    [laughs] So my flashlight is a pen with a sun in it.
ORANGE  [deadpan] Please never teach anyone anything.                                            (push-in)
BLUE    [suspicious] Then why does mine flicker when I shake it?
ORANGE  Usually a little rust on the contacts. Rub them with a pencil eraser.                     (INS: rust, eraser)
BLUE    [whispering] Or I just smack it.
ORANGE  [sighs] Eraser. And now you know.
BLUE    [curious] Hold on. Real talk...   (loops into the hook)
```

20 lines, 170 words. Slang: "real talk", "guts" (2). No negation triplet. No line starts with "Okay".
