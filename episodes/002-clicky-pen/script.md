```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 002  /  "HOW DOES A CLICKY PEN WORK?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). WS. Hand on the button. CLICK: the tip pops out.
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Why your pen stays out... after one click.

03.00  MECHANISM (3-12 s). Push in to MS. Callouts one at a time: SPRING, BUTTON, CAM, STOPS.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): No magnet. No motor. No glue.
        NARRATOR (5.4s): Just a spring on the ink tube,
        NARRATOR (7.5s): a button, a spinning cam,
        NARRATOR (9.8s): and fixed stops inside the barrel.

12.00  REVEALS (12-35 s). Click back in, then CU in slow motion. Cam pushed down, twists, springs up, catches on the short step. CLICK! pop, character recoils.
        NARRATOR (12.0s): Click it back in. Now, in slow motion.
        NARRATOR (14.5s): Press, and the button shoves the cam down, past the stops.
        NARRATOR (20.4s): Its angled teeth give it a little twist.
        NARRATOR (23.3s): Let go, and the spring pushes everything back up.
        NARRATOR (26.7s): The cam twists a bit more,
        NARRATOR (29.2s): and its ribs catch on a short step. Locked.
        NARRATOR (32.9s): Click!

35.00  FULL DEMO (35-50 s). MS. Click: long slot, tip in. Step, slot, step, slot. Bonus: the two clicks you hear.
        NARRATOR (35.0s): Click again. The cam twists again,
        NARRATOR (37.5s): and the ribs slide up a long slot.
        NARRATOR (40.0s): Short step, tip out. Long slot, tip in.
        NARRATOR (42.9s): Listen closely. One click going down,
        NARRATOR (45.8s): and a second one as it locks in place.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). WS, same as the opening.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): Your pen has no memory at all.
        NARRATOR (53.5s): A tiny cam just turns a little with every click.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). Hand back on the button, flows into the hook.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...

```
