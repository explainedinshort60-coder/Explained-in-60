# 018 Thermostat: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 018  /  "HOW DOES A THERMOSTAT KNOW WHEN TO TURN ON?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). Frame 1: wide room. A round wall thermostat behind a clear cover, its coil tip ON the contact,
        sparking; the wire glows with current dots; the furnace below roars (flames, warm air). CLICK! pops. Host points
        at it, grinning. Deep whoosh.
        NARRATOR (0.0s): This thermostat turns your heat on by bending.

03.00  COIL (3-6.25 s). Close on the dial: the spiral coil, brass inside, steel outside (COIL).
        NARRATOR (3.0s): Inside: a coil of brass and steel, stuck together.

06.25  STRIP (6.25-9.5 s). A close-up card: a straight two-layer strip (BRASS on top, STEEL below). Snowflakes; it
        curls up toward the brass side.
        NARRATOR (6.25s): Here's the kicker: brass shrinks more in the cold.

09.50  COLD (9.5-16 s). Wide: cold wisps drift past, badge 66°F, the host shivers. Close: the coil curls, the tip
        swings toward the contact (TIP). Gear ratchet.
        NARRATOR (9.5s): So when your room cools down,
        NARRATOR (12.5s): the coil curls tighter and tighter,

16.00  CONTACT (16-19.5 s). Push in: the tip lands on the contact (CONTACT). TINK. Spark.
        NARRATOR (16.0s): until its tip touches a metal contact.

19.50  FURNACE (19.5-23.5 s). Current dots run down the wire; the camera follows down to the furnace; flames whoosh on.
        NARRATOR (19.5s): Click. That closes a circuit, and the furnace fires up.

23.50  GAG (23.5-27.5 s). The host leans in, ear on the thermostat, listening. The click goes off in his ear: CLICK!,
        record scratch, boing, he recoils, then laughs and points.
        NARRATOR (23.5s): Yep. That tiny click is the whole brain.

27.50  OFF (27.5-35 s). Warm air rises from the vent; close: the coil loosens, the tip pulls away (TIP), the current
        stops, the flames die (slide whistle down).
        NARRATOR (27.5s): As the room warms, the coil loosens,
        NARRATOR (31.0s): the tip pulls away, and the heat shuts off.

35.00  DIAL (35-38.5 s). Close: the dial turns, carrying the contact one way then the other (DIAL).
        NARRATOR (35.0s): Turning the dial just slides the contact closer or farther.

38.50  BONUS (38.5-50 s). Wide: a highlight on the thermostat (FEELS THIS SPOT). Sun through the window, a beam on
        the dial, badge 80°F?, furnace off, the host shivers (BRRR.). Then a cold draft blows from the window, the tip
        hits the contact, the furnace roars, the host sweats and facepalms (TOO HOT.).
        NARRATOR (38.5s): Bonus: it only feels the air right next to it.
        NARRATOR (42.0s): Sun or a lamp on it? It thinks the house is warm.
        NARRATOR (46.0s): A cold draft? It runs the heat for nothing.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). Back to the opening composition: tip on the contact, spark, furnace roaring, host pointing.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So one curling metal strip runs your heat.
        NARRATOR (53.75s): Lowkey genius, right?

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). CLICK! pops back in at the frame 0 position.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```
