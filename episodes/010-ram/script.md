# 010 RAM: script

```
EXPLAINED IN 60  /  EP 010  /  "WHAT DOES RAM ACTUALLY DO?"

00.00  HOOK (0-3 s). Frame 1: host mid-yank, the plug in his fist, sparks at the socket, the monitor with
        essay_FINAL (UNSAVED tag) glitching. By 0.9 s the screen is black. Host recoils. Pop: NOOO!
        NARRATOR (0.0s): Cord yanked... and your work is just gone?

03.00  TURN (3-10 s). Back on, wider. A RAM stick pops up out of the tower (RAM). Host points at it.
        NARRATOR (3.0s): Blame this little stick. It's called RAM.
        NARRATOR (6.25s): It's your computer's main memory.

10.00  MECHANISM (10-19.75 s). Cut inside the chip: a 4x3 grid of cells on word lines and bit lines.
        Each capacitor is a water bucket (CAPACITOR).        NARRATOR (10.0s): Zoom in: billions of tiny buckets called capacitors.
        Close-up: FULL = 1, EMPTY = 0.                       NARRATOR (13.25s): Full bucket means one. Empty means zero.
        Close-up: the gates blink open (TRANSISTOR, click).  NARRATOR (16.5s): A tiny gate, a transistor, opens to fill or read it.

19.75  THE LEAK (19.75-26 s). Wide grid: every full bucket drips (LEAK). Levels sink, ones turn red and flip to 0 (FADING).
        NARRATOR (19.75s): Here's the kicker: every bucket leaks.
        NARRATOR (22.75s): Wait too long and your ones fade into zeros.

26.00  REFRESH (26-29.5 s). A cobalt bar sweeps the columns and tops every 1 back up (REFRESH, badge 64 ms, ding).
        NARRATOR (26.0s): So RAM tops up every bucket, about every 64 milliseconds.

29.50  GAG (29.5-33 s). Back at the desk: the host tries it himself with a watering can into a leaky bucket.
        Water jets out the holes, puddle grows on the floor, boing. Pop: TOO SLOW! (rimshot)
        NARRATOR (29.5s): By hand? No cap, impossible.

33.00  COUNTER (33-36.25 s). Grid: sweeps go blurry fast, counter REFILLS TODAY spins to 1,350,000 (ratchet, ding).
        NARRATOR (33.0s): RAM does it over a million times a day.

36.25  POWER OFF (36.25-43 s). Desk: host yanks the plug again, sparks, glitch, black screen.
        Grid goes grey, the sweep stops, every bucket drains, all digits 0 (NO REFILLS). Pop: BYE, ESSAY. (sad trombone)
        NARRATOR (36.25s): But pull the power, and the refills stop.
        NARRATOR (39.6s): Every bucket drains. Your work drains with it.

43.00  BONUS (43-50 s). Grid powered again, close-up: a READ lights one bit line, its bucket empties into it. Pop: SLURP!
        The refresh bar passes and fills it right back (REFILL, ding).
        NARRATOR (42.9s): Bonus: even reading a bucket drains it,
        NARRATOR (46.25s): so RAM refills it right after every read.

50.00  TAKEAWAY (50-57 s). Desk, same composition as the opening: plugged in, essay on screen.
        NARRATOR (50.0s): So RAM is your computer's main memory...
        NARRATOR (53.5s): Lightning fast, always leaking. Hit save.

57.00  LOOP OUT (57-60 s). The host reaches for the cord and yanks it: back to frame 0, flows into the hook.
        NARRATOR (57.0s): And now you know...
```

Words: 128 spoken. Slang: "here's the kicker", "no cap" (2).

## Craft rule (no negation list) and replacement line
- No "No X. No Y. No Z." anywhere (VO, captions, description). The takeaway is positive ("Lightning fast, always leaking. Hit save.").
- Replacement positive stakes line, shown: the "you've done this a thousand times" moment, unsaved work vanishing when the power cuts, is the hook itself (frame 1, mid-yank). First moving part at 0 s (sparks, glitch), RAM stick at 3.4 s, buckets at 10 s.

## Variation vs episodes 007, 008, 009 (need 3 of 6)
- Camera path: hard cuts between two worlds (desk and a full-screen chip grid), close-ups by cell, no magnifier bubble (008: bubble dive; 009 podcast: two-shot plus cutaways). CHANGED
- Host blocking: yanks a power cord twice, points, pours a watering can; host absent from the whole chip sequence (008: holds the phone the whole time). CHANGED
- Prop staging: desk set (monitor, tower, socket, cord) plus a schematic grid world (008: handheld phone and a floating inset). CHANGED
- Gag type: slapstick "do it by hand" failure, leaky bucket and puddle (008: snail race). CHANGED
- Color accent: green circuit board, gold contacts, cobalt refresh bar, red fading bits (008: amber syrup on icy blue). CHANGED
- Hook shape: action plus loss ("Cord yanked... and your work is just gone?"), mid-action first frame (008: battery % fail state). CHANGED (closest of the six; both are loss hooks)

## Better than 008
- Tighter hook: frame 1 is already mid-action (plug in fist, sparks, glitch), black screen at 0.9 s, "NOOO!" at 0.6 s.
- More visual change: two worlds, 22 camera cuts, drips, red flipping digits, sweeps, counter, power-off grey, read flow, two plug yanks.
- Funnier gag: the host tries to out-refill RAM with a watering can and floods the floor, pop TOO SLOW!, rimshot; plus the BYE, ESSAY. drain.
