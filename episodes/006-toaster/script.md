## Craft rule (no negation list) and replacement line
- No "No X. No Y. No Z." anywhere (VO, captions, description). The only "not" is one contrast ("it's not watching. It's counting."), not a list.
- Replacement positive stakes line, shown: "And one tiny magnet decides when your toast jumps." over a push-in on the magnet. First moving part on screen at 0.8 s (the rack pops).
