The 40-second format
Default length 40 seconds. Two changes from the old recipe: Orange says "nope" inside the first 5 seconds, and a red WRONG stamp lands the moment Blue speaks, so a viewer who swipes at 0:03 never leaves believing the myth. The 0:20 to 0:34 beat is now a second stake for the viewer, not more trivia.

Held in reserve: What If Blue...? (second-person extreme hypothetical, Blue as the test subject).
Titles use one of five frames: "Why [weird everyday thing]", "How To [fix your annoyance]", "Is [claim] Really True?", "What If You [extreme change]?", "How [store or system] Uses Your [money or body]". End with one reaction emoji.
Topic mix: about 70% stake topics; at most 30% pure trivia, kept as controls. Never repeat a topic (repeats flopped). Already used: toilet flush, toilet running, clicky pen, windshield frost, RAM, flashlight, toaster, phone battery in the cold, fridge light, zipper, escalator handrail.
Exports: clean master per platform, no TikTok watermark on Instagram or YouTube.
Track against our own baseline (about 20s watched of 60, PH/UK/ID top countries): viewed vs swiped away, average percent viewed (50%+ on the 40s cut), US in the top 3 countries, median views per Short (3x by week 4), subscribers per 1,000 views, and clicks from Shorts into the long-form.

Weekly long-form: one 12-minute 16:9 episode
Shorts bring in new viewers; a 12-minute episode is what turns them into subscribers and earns watch time from YouTube's home feed and suggested videos. One per week, YouTube only (TikTok and Instagram get chapter clips).
Format rules
One theme, four chapters. Each chapter grows out of a Short from the same week, so 3 to 4 minutes of animation already exists. New animation needed is about 6 to 7 minutes, not 12.
A story runs underneath. Blue has a goal (hosting, fixing, building something) and keeps getting it wrong; Orange's explanations save it. Without the story it's a playlist, and people leave after one chapter.
Cold open by 0:30 with the funniest failure and a promise: "by the end, you'll know more than ___."
Every chapter ends on an open question that the next chapter answers (re-hook every 2 to 3 minutes).
Blue's wrong theories counter stays on screen and is totaled at the end.
YouTube chapters with timestamps in the description; an end screen that points to the Shorts playlist and next week's episode.
Release: Saturday, slot B (8 pm ET, 5 pm PT; 08:00 WIB Sunday), so the week's Shorts have already teased it.
